About The Shop

Your device deserves more than a guess-and-check fix.

Shiv Shakti Mobile Repairing was built on a simple idea: repair work should be done by people who understand the hardware, not just swap parts and hope. Our workbench handles everything from a quick screen protector fit to full component-level motherboard rework under a microscope.
